- Fork, clone and mirror this repository, and build and run it,
  modified or not, for development, testing or contributing.
- Say that your project is based on KiwiCV, or link to it.
- Use the names to refer to the official service, accurately.

## What needs permission

- Offering a fork, modified or not, to the public as a service, or
  distributing it as a separate product, under the KiwiCV or
  KiwiCanopy name or with their logos.
- Using the names or logos in a way that suggests your project is
  the official one, or is endorsed by it.

## Rebranding a fork

A fork offered to the public replaces the name and logos wherever
they appear. The main places: `src/config/brand.ts`, the files in
`public/logos/kiwicv/`, `index.html` (metadata and structured data),
`public/manifest.json`, `netlify.toml`, and the locale files under
`src/locales/`. `git grep -i kiwi` finds the rest.
